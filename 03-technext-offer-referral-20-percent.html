What the 20% is based on

Where the money comes from

The reward is 20% of the TechNext implementation fee the referred company actually pays. Pass-through costs are left out, so the number is clean and easy to check.

Counts toward your 20%

  • Odoo implementation fee (setup, configuration, training)
  • Data migration and custom development in the first project
  • Change requests signed within 6 months of the first agreement

Does not count

  • Odoo subscription and user licences
  • Third-party apps, hosting, domains, hardware
  • Taxes (GST / VAT) and bank charges
  • Monthly support or retainer plans

Terms and conditions

Program terms

Draft terms for discussion. Tap a heading to expand.

1. Who can refer
  1. Open to existing TechNext clients with an active, paid engagement and no overdue invoices ("Referrer").
  2. Employees, contractors and official partners of TechNext are not eligible.
  3. A company cannot refer itself, its parent, its subsidiaries or companies under common control.
2. Valid referrals
  1. The referred company must be new to TechNext: not a client, and not an open lead or in talks with TechNext in the past 6 months.
  2. The referral must be registered through TechNext's referral form before TechNext's first contact with the company.
  3. A registration is valid for 90 days. If the company signs within 90 days, the reward applies. After that, the Referrer can re-register once.
  4. If two Referrers register the same company, the first complete registration wins.
3. Reward amount
  1. 20% of the implementation fees the referred company actually pays TechNext under its first implementation agreement, plus change requests signed within 6 months of that agreement.
  2. Excludes Odoo subscriptions and licences, third-party products and services, hosting, hardware, taxes, bank charges and support or retainer plans.
  3. Alternatively, the Referrer may choose service credit worth 25% of the same base, usable on TechNext services within 12 months. Credit has no cash value.
  4. No cap on the number of referrals.
4. Payment
  1. Rewards are paid in the currency of the referred project, within 30 days after TechNext receives each payment from the referred company.
  2. Payment by bank transfer to the Referrer's company account against an invoice or payment voucher from the Referrer.
  3. If the referred company receives a refund or credit note, the matching reward is deducted from future payouts or must be returned.
  4. Unpaid or written-off invoices from the referred company earn no reward.
5. The referred company's welcome gift
  1. A company that signs through this program gets its website built under TechNext's free website offer, subject to that offer's terms and slot availability.
  2. If free website slots run out, TechNext will offer an alternative welcome gift of similar value.
6. Conduct and disclosure
  1. The Referrer makes introductions only. The Referrer may not make promises about price, scope or timelines on TechNext's behalf.
  2. The Referrer should tell the referred company that it may receive a referral reward.
  3. Spam, paid ads using TechNext's name, or misleading claims void the reward.
7. Tax and compliance
  1. The Referrer is responsible for any tax due on rewards received.
  2. Where the Referrer's own company policy or public-sector rules forbid accepting rewards, the Referrer must not join or must choose to waive the reward.
8. Changes to the program
  1. TechNext may modify, pause or end this program and these terms at any time. Referrals already registered keep the terms in force at registration for 90 days.
  2. TechNext's records of registration date and payments received are final, unless shown to be in error.
  3. These terms are governed by the laws of Singapore.
